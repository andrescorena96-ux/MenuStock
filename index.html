About Creatussiness

Productiveness, without the burnout

Productiveness builds society through satisfaction and efficiency. Some spare time to have a drink is always good.

Productivity was never supposed to mean endless work. It's supposed to give people their time back. Efficiency should free up hours, not fill them — good software should remove unnecessary effort instead of adding to it, and a business tool should feel comfortable to use, day after day.

That's the standard we hold Creatussiness to: it should quietly contribute to your quality of life. Having a little time to breathe, rest, catch up with someone, or simply enjoy a drink isn't a distraction from a good workday — it's part of one.

Creatussiness isn't trying to make you work more. It's trying to make the unnecessary work take less time.

Where Creatussiness came from

A daughter's project for her dad

As a little girl, I had big dreams, like any kid does. Every day I tried to learn something new — tech, robotics, cars, flying, all of it. Haha.

But like any child, what I wanted most was my family's company, and even more so because my parents were my best friends. My dad was always busy, and I worried he never really got to rest the way he deserved, or feel good doing it. So much of his day went into hours of inventory work — and probably didn't help that the rest of his time went to a phone screen and its harsh, constant light.

So I decided to build him something. Not to make him work more — I wanted him to rest — but if he had to keep working, I at least wanted him to feel comfortable doing it. Warm colors help with that, especially when the light is soft. And I'll admit it: I love red, and it reminds him of me.

That's where Creatussiness was born — from wanting to give one person a little more time back, and realizing how many other entrepreneurs and workers needed the exact same thing.
